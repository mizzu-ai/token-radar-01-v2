Token Radar 01 (radar-2 Model Specification)

Token Radar is an autonomous intelligence system for evaluating pre-token DeFi protocols. It ingests sealed snapshot metrics to compute implied pre-launch valuations and generate concrete farming action plans.

Value Model Architecture

base               = TVL × categoryMultiple
growthPct          = clamp(change_7d ?? 0, -50, 150)
growthMult         = 1 + (growthPct / 100) × 1.2
age_days           = as_of − listed_at (days; default 365)
maturityMult       = 0.7 + min(age_days, 1460) / 1460 × 0.6   [range 0.7 – 1.3]
chainMult          = 1 + min(max(chains.length − 1, 0), 5) × 0.03  [range 1.0 – 1.15]
implied_value_usd  = max(1, round(base × growthMult × maturityMult × chainMult))

Category Multiples & Structural Rationale

  • Dexs (0.35): Market makers capture real onchain trading volume and swap fees directly.
  • Lending & Risk Curators & Derivatives (0.30): Core financial building blocks with sticky borrow interest and perpetual margin turnover.
  • Yield / Yield Aggregator & Capital Allocator (0.28): Capital-efficient automated yield aggregators.
  • Liquidity Manager & Leveraged Farming (0.25): Concentrated liquidity infrastructure and leveraged farming mechanisms.
  • RWA & Basis Trading (0.22): Offchain asset custody and delta-neutral funding capture.
  • CDP & Stablecoins (0.08): Low multiple: the token IS the stable product or minted asset; there is no separate secondary token launch required to extract value.
  • Other Categories (0.20) / Null (0.18): Standard defensive baseline.

Growth Outranking Dynamics

Because the growth multiplier scales up to 2.8x (+150% growth: 1 + 1.5×1.2 = 2.8x) while dropping to 0.40x at -50% contraction, a fast-growing breakout protocol can surpass a substantially larger stagnating protocol.
